# Trim and transmission clues in photos

Use this when looking at a listing's photos. Photos can be stock images or of a
different car, so these are clues, not proof. Write what you saw, not what you
expect to see.

## Subaru BRZ, 2017 to 2020, Canada

| Trim | Clues |
|---|---|
| Base | Manual only. Cloth seats. Single-zone climate. Key start. 17-inch alloys. 7-inch touchscreen. |
| Sport-tech | Automatic only in 2019 and 2020. Black leather and Alcantara seats with red stitching. Heated seat switches on the console. Dual-zone climate with an AUTO button. Push-button start. LED fog lights. |
| Sport-tech RS | Manual. Sport-tech equipment plus Brembo calipers (logo visible through the wheels), SACHS shocks, gunmetal grey 17-inch wheels. |
| tS (2018) | STI badging, Brembo brakes, black rear wing, STI wheels. About 100 in Canada. |
| Editions | Raiu, Inazuma, Hikari, Aozora: special paint and badging. |

## Toyota 86, 2017 to 2020, Canada

| Trim | Clues |
|---|---|
| Base | Cloth seats. Plain climate knobs without AUTO. No rear spoiler. No fog lights. |
| GT (2018 on) | Seats with suede-like inserts and leather bolsters. Dual-zone automatic climate. Passive keyless entry. Rear spoiler. LED fog lights. Multi-information display in the gauges. |
| TRD Special Edition (2019) | Built on the GT. Manual only. Brembo brakes, SACHS dampers, 18-inch wheels, red stitching and red seat belts, TRD trim, Raven Black paint. 50 in Canada. |
| Hakone Edition (2020) | Built on the GT. Hakone green paint, bronze 17-inch wheels, black spoiler, tan and black Alcantara seats. |

## Scion FR-S, 2013 to 2016

One trim. Release Series cars have special paint and numbered plaques.

## GR86 and BRZ, 2022 on

Base or Premium (GR86) and Sport-tech (BRZ). Premium and Sport-tech have leather and Ultrasuede heated seats and 18-inch wheels.

## Transmission

- Automatic: a P R N D gate on the shifter, a gear letter (P, D) in the gauges, paddle shifters behind the steering wheel.
- Manual: an H-pattern knob with numbers, three pedals, a gear position not shown as P or D.

## Mods to note

Aftermarket wheels, wings and aero, exhausts and tips, intakes, coilovers or a lowered stance, aftermarket shift knobs or pedals, turbo or supercharger parts in engine bay photos, warning lights on the dash.

Sources: Subaru Canada 2019 BRZ pricing (auto123.com, 2018), Toyota Canada 2019 86 pricing (autotrader.ca editorial, August 2018), Toyota Canada 2020 Hakone Edition release.
