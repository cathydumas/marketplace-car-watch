How the calls work
Market
What similar cars ask, from real AutoTrader.ca and Facebook listings, adjusted for year, km, transmission, trim and model. There is no free Canadian blue book.
Buy
Asking price is at least 7% under similar cars.
Offer
Close to market. The number is a suggested opening offer, lowered for cars that have sat for a month or more.
Pass
More than 10% over similar cars, or a blown engine, a rebuilt title or similar.
Flip check
Likely resale after 18 months of about 12,000 km a year, before tax, insurance and repairs.
From photos
Claude looks at each promising car's photos for trim clues (seat material, climate controls, screen, brakes, wheels), the shifter and paddles to confirm manual or automatic, and mods the ad leaves out. Photos can be stock images, so treat this as a clue, not proof.
Title
Cars whose ad says rebuilt, salvage or branded are dropped. "Clean title only" shows cars whose ad or Facebook listing says clean title. A seller's word is not proof: check the ICBC or Carfax report.
Other ratings
CarGurus and AutoTrader rate the asking price only. They ignore condition, mods and dealer fees.
Not checked
Accident history, condition and ownership. Every car still needs a history report and a pre-purchase inspection.
